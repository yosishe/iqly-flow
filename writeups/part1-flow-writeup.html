Decision. A disclosed post-quiz account gate: the true correct-answer count is free; a free email-only account reveals the illustrative IQ range and rank, full review and history.

Metric. Account creations / page visits is the brief's objective. Seven-day verified accounts per eligible new visitor is my proposed production shipping metric.

Strongest alternative. Score-first gives the range and rank before the account. It remains Test 1 because the control is a judgment under uncertainty.

The decision: a visible exchange

My first control gave the range free. I changed to a disclosed gate after examining how an optional account might lose its reason to exist once the result is delivered. Historical research found several competitor architectures, but no comparable conversion data. A hypothetical 40% versus 12% account rate among finishers makes score-first require 3.33 times the upstream starts × completion to tie. That arithmetic exposes an assumption; it does not estimate either rate or prove the gate superior. I kept the exchange explicit: no payment, the account boundary before question one, and a true partial result before the ask. Whether review and history earn an email remains untested.

What the result can support

I chose a labelled range rather than a precise IQ score to represent the brief's product concept. The question bank has no measured reliability, standard error or population norms; figures from another test cannot validate it. Correct answers are counted deterministically. The range, rank and curve are explicitly illustrative mappings, and area counts are not an ability profile. Retakes can reflect practice. “~5 min” is an estimate; no timer adds pressure to these untimed tasks. Neither choice has a measured conversion advantage.

What works and what is simulated

The account syntax-checks an email and stores it locally when possible; no email is sent, ownership verified or server account created. Blocked storage is disclosed. Review, attempt history and deletion work; email sign-in and a population rank would be future services. Events exclude the typed email. Current Chrome checks cover all four flows, state recovery, zero-correct attempts and six viewport widths; physical-phone checks remain a human step.
