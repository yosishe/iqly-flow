# IQly — Decision log

**Final authority: 10 September 2026.** Rows 1–39 below record historical judgments and measurements, including superseded choices. They do not establish conversion performance or current QA. The control reversal is preserved as a decision under uncertainty. Current behavior, metrics and shipping rules are in README.md, MEASUREMENT-SPEC.md and EXPERIMENT-CONTRACTS.md; current verification is in QA-CHECKLIST.md. Historic fold offsets, Linux results, deadlines and old sample-size approximations are not fresh evidence. No deadline was found in the official pages or inspected outgoing email thread.

**Corrections after reconstructing the original evidence:** the competitor table was a mixed set of observations, not 19 completed comparable funnels; the 16/19 prevalence assertion is withdrawn. Reliability and measurement error from other tests cannot be assigned to this bank, even with an inference label. Disclosure, precision and account-demand effects remain hypotheses. Email sign-in and an updating population rank are future functionality. A few interviews can test comprehension, not establish account demand. Codex identified the final race, malformed-state, deletion-reachability, rendering and analysis defects; the user authorized the corrections. The final public checkout is canonical; private preparation no longer generates it.
